## How do people brew instant coffee in Indian cities?

In most Indian city kitchens, instant coffee starts with a kettle or a microwave mug. Some pour hot water straight over the granules and stir; others swear by the 'bloom' method — a splash of hot water first, a quick swirl, then the rest of the cup. Milk drinkers often heat milk on the stove, froth it with a small whisk, and fold in the coffee at the end. A friend in Bangalore keeps a French press on her desk and uses it for instant on busy mornings — she says the plunge gives a silkier mouthfeel. If you like it black, try dissolving the granules in a teaspoon of room-temperature water before adding hot water; it prevents clumping and brings out more aroma.
