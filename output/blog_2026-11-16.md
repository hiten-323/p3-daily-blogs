## How do people brew instant coffee in Indian cities?

Walk into any apartment kitchen in Bangalore or Delhi at 7 a.m. and the ritual looks surprisingly similar: a steel tumbler, a spoon that has seen thousands of stirs, and a jar of instant coffee within arm's reach. Most people just boil water in an electric kettle, drop in a heaped spoonful, add sugar if that's their thing, and pour in hot milk straight from the packet. Some swear by the 'pull' — pouring the mixture back and forth between tumbler and davara until a thin layer of foam crowns the surface. Others keep it quiet: mug, microwave, thirty seconds, done. The real difference shows up in what's inside the jar. If your morning cup tastes flat, try heating the milk just until tiny bubbles form at the edges — not a full boil — and stir the coffee into a splash of hot water first.
