What does freeze-dried instant coffee mean for an Indian kitchen?

Freeze-dried coffee skips the high-heat spray towers that strip aroma from most supermarket jars. Instead, the brew is frozen solid and the ice is lifted away under vacuum, so the volatile oils that carry chocolate, caramel, and fruit notes stay locked in the granule. For an Indian kitchen that means the cup you stir together at 6 a.m. still smells like the roastery, not like burnt toast. A practical tip: use water just off the boil — around 92 °C — and let the granules sit for ten seconds before stirring; the cooler splash protects the delicate top notes that boiling water would blow off.
