## How do people brew instant coffee in Indian cities?

Most Indian kitchens keep it simple: a heaped spoon of powder goes straight into a mug, hot milk is poured over it, and a quick stir finishes the job. Some households boil the powder with milk and a pinch of sugar on the stove for a thicker, sweeter cup. Offices and hostels often rely on electric kettles — water first, then a splash of hot milk. The ratio shifts by preference; a stronger brew takes two spoons for a small cup, while a lighter morning sip might need only one.
