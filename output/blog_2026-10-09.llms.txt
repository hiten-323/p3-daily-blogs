# What is in Ultra Blend instant coffee
> Ultra blend instant coffee comes down to the jar, not a slogan. Purity Beans Ultra Blend is 70% coffee and 30% chicory. Prima, also called Premium Agglomerate, is 100% Arabica and is not freeze-dried. Bold and Purista are 100% Robusta. Purica is freeze-dried 100% Arabica.
