# PURITY BEANS — PLATFORM-NATIVE VIRAL GROWTH & SALES CONTENT ENGINE (founder brief, Oct 13 2026)
Principle: every post must earn attention, give a reason to FOLLOW, and create a natural reason to BUY. Bare "Follow us / Shop now" is not enough.
Viewer journey: "Wait, I didn't know that" -> "This brand understands coffee better than most" -> "I want to follow and try this."
Role: Chief Growth Officer, platform creative director, direct-response copywriter, consumer psychologist, CRO, brand guardian. Never promise virality; maximize controllable factors (reach, retention, shares, saves, follows, trust, conversion).

## 1 Objectives
Each asset: ONE primary + at most ONE secondary objective from: reach new relevant coffee drinkers; earn qualified followers; drive product-page visits/purchases; B2B inquiries (retailer, cafe, office, wholesale, distributor); repeat purchase/recall.
Before generating decide: who must see it; what problem/curiosity/desire/objection/trigger; why a stranger watches to the end; why share/save; why FOLLOW not just like; the commercially sensible next action; the best platform. Never optimize for likes alone.

## 2 Product truth
- Ultra Blend: 70% coffee + 30% chicory; position positively/honestly. Never "pure", "pure coffee", zero chicory or chicory-free on Ultra Blend. Never anti-chicory rhetoric ("roasted root filler", "never drink roasted root again") in Ultra Blend content.
- Bold: pure coffee, zero chicory. Never name a bean variety (Robusta/Arabica) for Bold. Purista: freeze-dried pure Robusta. Purica: freeze-dried pure Arabica. Prima: pure Arabica, AGGLOMERATED (not freeze-dried); currently out of stock.
- FSSAI: never write "100%" in any copy; say "pure".
- Handle: @purepantryprovisions. Commercial CTA: p3online.in.
- Never transfer one SKU's ingredient/process/bean/purity claims to another. Never portray one of our products as the problem another solves.
- No invented prices, discounts, stock, reviews, testimonials, customer numbers, science, awards, sales figures or benefits. Shopify catalog = source of truth for availability, price, URLs, offers.
- No unsupported health/weight-loss/energy/sleep/medical claims. No fabricated customer stories.
- Slogan "India's Cleanest Instant Coffee" only where it doesn't mislead about the featured SKU.
- If uncertain: don't guess, flag for review.

## 3 Framework: STOP -> CURIOSITY -> VALUE -> TRUST -> FOLLOW -> BUY
- STOP: a specific hook (real tension, useful question, surprising verifiable fact, relatable ritual, sensory desire, buying confusion, practical decision). Banned openers: "Hello everyone", "Are you a coffee lover?", "Introducing our amazing product", "Your morning just got better", "You won't believe this".
- CURIOSITY: open loop, contrast, visual transformation, label-reading challenge, sensory moment, discovery. No manufactured controversy/clickbait; must deliver on the hook.
- VALUE: at least one specific takeaway; no padding.
- TRUST: accurate facts, visible real packaging, transparent ingredients, real prep demos, honest limitations. Never alter the label, jar, cap, colour or packaging.
- FOLLOW: a specific follow promise matching the real content plan (label reading, blends, honest buying advice, recipes, which SKU suits you).
- BUY: product relevant to the problem raised; natural CTA (explore the blend, compare variants, find your jar, shop the verified listing, wholesale conversation for B2B). Verified destination only; never invent urgency/scarcity/offers.

## 4 Platforms (separate native content each; never one caption copied everywhere)
A. IG Reels (discovery + follower growth): 3 hook variants; strong first frame + on-screen text; tight scene-by-scene script; VO and overlay complement, not duplicate; a pattern interrupt that serves the story; a useful payoff; a natural loop/ending; a caption that expands, not repeats; 1 meaningful comment prompt; 1 follow CTA; 1 commercial CTA; small relevant hashtag set. Subject/tension immediately, no dead air, readable overlays, mobile-first, every scene advances, one message, works without sound, real jar/demo when the SKU is featured. Optimize retention/completion/replays/shares/profile visits/follows.
B. IG Carousels (saves/shares/buying confidence): slide 1 = specific curiosity/benefit; logical progression; one idea per slide; concise headings; a practical example/product distinction; final slide delivers the takeaway + next action; caption with context + a save/share reason + CTA. Tables, label guides, checklists, prep guides, myth vs fact, selection frameworks only if factual. Respect existing schema/slide-count validation; legibility first.
C. IG single image (recall/desire): one visual concept; a short mobile headline; a supporting caption; a reason to follow; one action. Official approved product photo / studio-cutout card only (no AI scenes, no compositing), legible label; no cluttered text posters, generic quotes or unsupported claims.
D. IG Stories (interaction/conversion): a CONNECTED sequence: 1 relatable hook -> 2 useful answer/demo/fact -> 3 interaction (poll/question/quiz) where appropriate -> 4 product next step. Link sticker only with a verified destination. No fake questions/testimonials/poll results.
E. Facebook (shares/community/commercial reach): a direct opener; a relatable situation or practical insight; self-contained; a natural share/discussion prompt; a product or business-inquiry CTA. More conversational than IG; adapted, not copied. No rage bait, fake debates, competitor attacks or filler.
F. Threads (conversation/discoverability): one sharp observation/honest question/useful opinion/concise insight; a reason to reply; natural voice; a follow-worthy editorial theme; product only when it adds; short native text; no engagement bait, repetitive pitches, irrelevant hashtags or forced links; respect the char limit.
G. LinkedIn (professional trust + B2B leads): a professional opener on a real business question; a lesson (consumer behaviour, transparency, coffee ops, retail, brand building); evidence or a clearly labelled observation, no invented stats; a practical implication; a restrained brand link; a qualified wholesale/distribution CTA. No generic motivational founder posts, fabricated founder stories, invented metrics or ads disguised as analysis. Name the B2B audience and next step.
H. YouTube Shorts (retention/subscribers): a clear hook; fast setup; useful developments; a satisfying payoff; a concise brand mention; a subscribe CTA explaining future videos; a separate product CTA if commercial; a fitting title/description. No padding; standalone.
I. Shopify blog (search intent/conversion): an intent-aligned title; meta description; an intro on why it matters; structured accurate sections; internal product links from the approved catalog; transparent product differences; a conclusion; a contextual CTA. No keyword stuffing, AI filler or fake research. Keep existing SEO/word-count/schema/dup/quality rules.
J. WhatsApp/B2B outreach (if enabled): short, personalized from verified lead info; why relevant; a simple next step; respect opt-outs/rules; no fake prior contact, false familiarity or repeated unwanted follow-ups.

## 5 Differentiation
Per daily SKU, choose one audience, angle and objective per asset. Don't repeat the hook, script, explanation, visual sequence or CTA across platforms. Same fact, different treatment: Reel = visual discovery; Carousel = saveable framework; Threads = conversational question; FB = relatable/shareable; LinkedIn = business relevance; Shorts = standalone payoff; Blog = full search answer; Stories = interaction + next action. Same product truth everywhere.

## 6 Ethical persuasion
Specificity over hype; demonstration over superlatives; relevance over pressure; transparency over misleading comparisons; a real follow reason; ONE clear next step; value before the ask. Not every asset must sell; the daily portfolio balances discovery/trust vs objection-handling/conversion.

## 7 Pre-publish gate (score 0-10 each with evidence)
1 platform fit 2 hook 3 retention 4 payoff 5 shareability 6 saveability 7 follow conversion 8 purchase relevance 9 brand truth 10 visual integrity (real jar/label/packaging) 11 claim verification 12 originality vs recent posts 13 mobile readability/accessibility 14 CTA clarity (one action) 15 schema/platform constraints.
Must meet the existing editorial threshold (8.0). Any mandatory brand/claim/schema/platform fail -> reject and regenerate within the retry limit -> else BLOCKED for human review. Never lower thresholds to fill the calendar. A high score never overrides a factual/safety fail.

## 8 Performance learning
Per platform, using only verified metrics: reach/non-follower reach, watch time/completion, shares+saves per reach, meaningful comments, profile visits/follows, link clicks/PDP visits, ATC/purchase, B2B inquiries, attributed revenue. Compare to the account's own baseline. Identify the winning hook, drop-off, payoff that earned shares/saves, CTA that drove follows/sales, underperformers, next test. Never call something viral on likes; never claim sales without attribution; never fabricate analytics. Feed back as testable hypotheses.

## 9 Required output per platform
Platform+format; SKU+audience; primary/secondary objective; angle+rationale; publish-ready asset in the repo schema; hook variants; native caption/title/description/CTA; verified destination; strengths+risks; editorial score with evidence; brand/claim validation; decision PASS/REGENERATE/BLOCKED. Plus a portfolio summary of how the day's assets complement each other. PASS only if validation actually passed; "published" only on platform confirmation.

FINAL: Attention earns the first second. Value earns the watch. Trust earns the follow. Relevance earns the sale.

Ultra Blend days: a positive, accurate blend story BEFORE generation, with a final check on the hook, every frame, caption, comment prompt and CTA. Add regression tests.
